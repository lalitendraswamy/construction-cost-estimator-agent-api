# Payment stages and construction timeline

> PLACEHOLDER: Ravikumar to supply the firm's actual payment schedule.

## Typical payment stages
Payments are linked to completed stages: agreement and mobilisation, foundation, each slab, brickwork, plastering, flooring and finishing, and handover. Each stage is invoiced only after it is completed and inspected.

## Typical timeline
A G+1 independent house usually takes about nine to twelve months from foundation to handover, depending on approvals, weather (monsoon), material availability and design changes during construction.
