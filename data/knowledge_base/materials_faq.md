## Cement grades
OPC 53 grade gains strength fast and is common for RCC slabs and columns. PPC is popular for plastering and masonry because it is more workable and resists cracks better in coastal conditions like Visakhapatnam.

## TMT steel
Fe-500 and Fe-500D TMT bars are standard for residential RCC. The "D" variant has higher ductility, useful in seismic zones. Buy from brands with mill test certificates.

## AAC blocks vs red bricks
AAC blocks are lighter, larger and insulate better, which reduces load on the structure and speeds up work. Red bricks are familiar, strong in compression and easy to repair. Both work well when the mortar and curing are done properly.
