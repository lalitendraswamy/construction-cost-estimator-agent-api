# Building approvals in Visakhapatnam (GVMC / VUDA / Panchayat)

> PLACEHOLDER: Ravikumar to supply the verified process, fees and documents checklist.

## Which authority applies
Plots inside Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation limits (for example MVP Colony, Madhurawada, Gajuwaka, Seethammadhara) need GVMC building permission. Plots in the wider metropolitan region outside GVMC limits fall under the Visakhapatnam Metropolitan Region Development Authority (VMRDA, formerly VUDA). Village plots outside both need gram panchayat permission, usually with layout approval from the development authority.

## What VUDA approval means
VUDA/VMRDA approval confirms that a layout or building plan follows the master plan, zoning, setbacks and road-width rules. Buyers and banks check it before loans and registration.

## Typical documents
Registered sale deed, link documents, encumbrance certificate, approved layout copy, site plan and building drawings signed by a licensed engineer, structural stability certificate for larger buildings, and identity proof of the owner.

## Timeline
Online applications are processed through the state building permission portal. Simple residential plans typically take a few weeks when documents are complete.
