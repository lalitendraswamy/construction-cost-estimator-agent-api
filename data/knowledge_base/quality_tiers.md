# Quality tiers: Basic, Standard, Premium

> PLACEHOLDER: Ravikumar to confirm brands and specifications per tier.

## Basic
Economical, durable construction. Standard-grade cement and TMT steel, red brick or fly-ash brick walls, vitrified floor tiles, basic sanitary fittings, emulsion paint.

## Standard
Branded mid-range materials. Branded cement and Fe-500 TMT steel, larger vitrified tiles, branded sanitary and CP fittings, modular electrical switches, better doors and windows.

## Premium
High-end finishes. Premium brands, granite or marble flooring in key areas, designer sanitary fittings, concealed wiring with premium switches, teak or engineered-wood main door, UPVC windows, false ceiling in living areas.
