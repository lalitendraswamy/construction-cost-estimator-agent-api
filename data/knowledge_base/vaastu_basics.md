# Vaastu basics

> PLACEHOLDER: short and non-prescriptive; ties to the Vaastu Analyzer product.

## Common preferences
Many families prefer an east- or north-facing main entrance, the kitchen in the south-east, the master bedroom in the south-west, and the puja room in the north-east. Staircases are often placed in the south or west.

## Practical note
Vaastu preferences can usually be accommodated at the design stage without extra construction cost. Use the Vaastu Analyzer for a plot-specific check.
